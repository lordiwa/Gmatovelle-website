## A. Audit of the current implementation

### A1. What is solid (keep as is)

- **Static HTML with full content.** `scripts/prerender.js` writes complete HTML per route plus 404, so crawlers that do not run JavaScript still see the content and the JSON-LD. The FAQ answers are always in the DOM (`src/components/FaqList.vue:14`, native details/summary), so they are extractable.
- **One source for head data.** `src/seo/meta.js` feeds both the prerender and the client, so the two cannot drift.
- **Canonical and hreflang logic is correct.** Self-referencing canonical per language, reciprocal es-EC / en / x-default set (`meta.js:164-173`). This matches Google's rules: reciprocal, self-referential, x-default for the fallback (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions).
- **Robots meta** `index,follow,max-snippet:-1,max-image-preview:large` (`meta.js:209`) leaves snippet and image preview unrestricted. That is the right posture for AI Overviews, which depend on snippet eligibility.
- **JSON-LD graph is linked by @id** (WebSite, ProfilePage, Physician, FAQPage). `medicalSpecialty: 'Psychiatric'` is a valid schema.org enum value. Phone is in E.164. Schema.org places Physician under both MedicalBusiness/LocalBusiness and MedicalOrganization (https://schema.org/Physician), so the type choice is sound.
- **Retired claims are test-locked** (`tests/unit/positioning.spec.js`), which protects the client's constraints.
- **Hosting config is sane.** `cleanUrls: true`, `trailingSlash: false` match the canonicals. Hashed assets are immutable. HTML is revalidated (`firebase.json:5-16`).
- **Fonts are self-hosted** with `font-display: swap` and unicode-range subsets (verified in `dist/assets/index-*.css`), so only the latin subsets download for normal Spanish and English text.
- **Hero portrait** has `fetchpriority="high"` (`ProfilePortrait.vue:52`). Other photos are lazy.

## C. Facts vs myths (GEO)

## D. Open questions for the client (consolidated)

1. **Domain.** Which domain will be bought, and who holds DNS? (Blocks item 1 and all measurement.)
2. **Email.** Is `info@gmail.com` real? What is the confirmed practice email? (D2.)
3. **Opening hours.** Days and times of consultation.
4. **Languages.** Does he consult in English? If so, can the site and listing say it (`knowsLanguage`, English query capture)?
5. **New patients.** Is he currently accepting new patients? (`isAcceptingNewPatients`.)
6. **Name and specialty wording.** The Google listing says "Médico Psiquiatra"; the site says "Médico Neuropsiquiatra". Which wording should appear on the listing? Does he want the Google listing renamed to match?
7. **External profiles.** Which of these exist and may be linked: LinkedIn, Asociación Ecuatoriana de Psiquiatría page, WFSBP/WPA directory entries, Universidad Central faculty page, psiquiatria.com listing, any Doctoralia-type listing in Ecuador (I could not confirm Doctoralia operates in Ecuador), ORCID, Google Scholar, PubMed, the publisher page for his book?
8. **Registry numbers.** Does he agree to publish his title and practice registration numbers for verifiability?
9. **Neighbourhood.** Is the office in Cumbayá, and does he want that named?
10. **AI training consent.** Does he accept that GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended may use the site content for model training? (Currently allowed explicitly. Citation does not require it; search bots are separate.)
11. **Publications.** Year and ISBN for the two books, and a link to any article list, to build Book nodes properly.
12. **Articles.** When will he have real articles, and who will review medical content? Does he know of any Ecuadorian rule on medical advertising that affects wording (I found none online; check with his professional college)?

## E. Measurement protocol

1. **Google Search Console**, Domain property via DNS. Submit the sitemap. Use URL Inspection to confirm Googlebot sees one canonical and the JSON-LD after item 3. Check the Generative AI performance report when available.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: import the site from Search Console, submit the sitemap, review AI Performance monthly (CSV).
3. **Google Business Profile** insights: calls, direction requests, searches by "psiquiatra".
4. **Manual citation audit, monthly.** About 20 prompts, ES and EN, split into three groups:
   - Branded: "Dr. Gonzalo Matovelle", "Gonzalo Matovelle psiquiatra Quito".
   - Local intent: "psiquiatra en Quito", "neuropsiquiatra Quito", "psiquiatra Quito trastorno bipolar", "psiquiatra Cumbayá".
   - Informational: "diferencia entre psiquiatra y psicólogo", "past presidente Asociación Ecuatoriana de Psiquiatría".
   Run each in ChatGPT (search on), Perplexity, Gemini, Claude (web search on), Copilot, plus Google AI Mode. Record: mentioned (y/n), linked (y/n), URL cited, claim accuracy, date. Repeat in a clean session, because results vary by location and session.
5. **Referrals**: in analytics look for referrals from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, copilot.microsoft.com and gemini.google.com. User agents are not visible on Firebase Hosting without extra setup (UNVERIFIED whether raw request logs are available); a CDN in front would expose them.
6. **Accuracy watch**: when an AI answer states a wrong fact (phone, address, retired claims), fix the sources it cites first (GBP, directories), then the site.
